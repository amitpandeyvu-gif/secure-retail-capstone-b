Our story

Clothing for the culture you love.

Pop Culture Clothing is a fictional Australian retail concept created for Capstone B. It combines expressive everyday design with a practical, database-backed shopping experience.

About the store

Designed to make shopping straightforward.

Our catalogue brings graphic tees, comfortable layers and useful accessories into one responsive store. Customers can search, compare live stock, choose product options, save favourites and complete a safe demonstration checkout.

The customer storefront and administration portal share one SQLite database. This means product availability, orders, payments and tracking information remain consistent across both sides of the system.
