Customer information

Delivery, returns and sizing.

Clear demonstration policies for the complete Capstone B customer journey.

DeliveryReturnsSizingPayments

Delivery

Standard delivery

Demonstration shipping costs AUD 9.95 and becomes free when the product subtotal reaches AUD 80.

Tracking

Use the Track button with your order number and checkout email. Administrators can update orders from Confirmed through Delivered.

Returns

Change of mind

This assessment store does not dispatch real products. A production version would provide a defined return window and condition requirements.

Cancelled orders

The administration portal can mark a demonstration order as cancelled, and that change appears in customer tracking.

Sizing

Choose an option

Each product page displays its size guide and the available size and colour combinations held in SQLite.

Stock accuracy

Checkout verifies stock again inside a transaction to prevent an unavailable option from being ordered.

Payments

Safe demonstration

No real card number is requested or stored. Successful checkouts create fictional payment records so the complete data relationship can be demonstrated safely.
