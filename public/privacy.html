Privacy policy.

How this assessment storefront handles account, order and support information.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Information we use

When you use Google sign-in, Pop Culture Clothing receives your verified email address and display name from Google. We use them to create or retrieve your demonstration customer account, save a shopping bag and associate demonstration orders with you.

How information is stored

Catalogue, account, cart, order and support data are stored in the application database. The hosted Vercel demonstration uses temporary serverless storage and may reset. The browser stores basic session, bag and wishlist information so the interface can work between page visits.

Google authentication

Google authenticates you directly. This website does not receive or store your Google password. The server verifies the signed Google identity token and uses only the account details needed for sign-in.

Payments and email

No real card details are requested or stored. Payment references are fictional. If sign-in notifications are enabled, your email address is sent to the configured email delivery provider solely to deliver that notification.

Sharing and retention

Information is not sold. It is shared only with service providers needed to operate authentication, hosting and optional email delivery. Demonstration records may be removed when the hosted database resets.

Your choices

You may sign out at any time and revoke the app’s Google access from your Google Account. For questions or removal requests, use the contact page.
